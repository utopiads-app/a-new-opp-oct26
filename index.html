90 day roadmap

Before launch

Confirm eligibility with OpenAI, set up Ads Manager, install and test the OpenAI pixel on ashcroft.com and sales.ashcroft.com.

Month 1

Launch on the Clicks objective. Weekly review of click through rate, cost per click, engagement and devices.

Month 2

Move to the Conversions objective once quote requests are recording. Compare lead quality with Google Ads.

Month 3

Evaluate against the success criteria and decide to scale, refine or pause.

Scale

$140 or less per qualified lead

Increase budget and consider adding Canada.

Refine

$140 to $250 per qualified lead

Continue with revised ads, context hints and landing pages.

Pause

Above $250, or no qualified leads after 60 days

Move the budget to Google Search.

Eligibility comes first. OpenAI's ad policy (updated September 10, 2026) says ads are mainly limited to consumer categories during the initial period, and industrial products are not currently listed. Ashcroft's eligibility will be confirmed with OpenAI before any build work begins.

Recommendation and next steps

We recommend Scenario B: an increase of $3,500 a month (about 30%), for a 2027 total of $182,499 and about 579 leads a month.

  • Confirm the 2027 budget level.
  • January 2027: apply Step 1 and the Scenario B Search increases.
  • January to March 2027: run the ChatGPT Ads pilot, with a decision at the end of March.
  • Each quarter: compare results with this forecast and rebalance toward the best performing campaigns.
  • Revisit Microsoft Bing Ads once lead tracking is restored; before the tracking issue, Bing delivered leads at $13.66 each.
